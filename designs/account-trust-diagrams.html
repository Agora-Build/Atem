One pairing, many devices

§9 proposal for Atem + Astation — same account, no repeated pairing, no forwarding through the Mac, no trust in the account login. Full text: account-trust.md

The idea

The account keeps a small signed device list. Atem pins its start when it pairs once, then accepts exactly the devices that list names — whoever relays the traffic.

Paironce per Atem, with any one trusted Astation (today's safety-code ceremony).
Add a deviceonce per device: an already-trusted device approves it with Touch ID / password.
Connectany listed device reaches every Atem of the account, over relay or direct :8080.
RemoveSettings → Devices → Remove. Atem stops accepting it within 24 h at most (usually at once).
Not trustedthe account login, the relay, presence, or a device's own claims.
Not neededthe Mac staying online, or pairing each device with each Atem.

Who holds what

Each device keeps its own key. The relay only stores the list; signatures make it trustworthy.

Adding the iPhone — once

The approval is a signature by a trusted device after the user compares a code and authenticates. Logging in alone produces nothing Atem accepts.

  participant A as Atem (any host)
  U->>P: sign in to the same account

the next time any device connects or it polls the head

The iPhone connects to Atem

Atem checks the chain from its pin, a fresh head, and that the device really holds its key — then grants exactly its roles.

  Note over P,A: Step 2 of the proposal: both derive session keys from the ephemeral exchange
so the relay can't read terminal or chat traffic

Removing a device, and staying safe offline

Removal is just another signed entry. Atem never moves backwards and never trusts a list older than 24 h.
